This full-day workshop explores the emerging paradigm of programmatic representations—symbolic programs, code-based policies, and rule-based abstractions—to address fundamental challenges in reinforcement learning (RL) such as interpretability, generalizability, efficiency, and safety. By bringing together experts in RL, imitation learning, planning, and program synthesis, the workshop aims to advance autonomous systems capable of complex decision-making in diverse environments. Specific topics include using programs as policies, reward functions, task generators, and environment models, drawing on recent advances in areas like PIRL, Eureka, and GenSim. This approach enables human-understandable reasoning, reduces reliance on massive data-driven models, and promotes modularity, fostering progress toward verifiable and robust agents across virtual and real-world applications.
